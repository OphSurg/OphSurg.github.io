Overview

Ophthalmic-specific simulation and learning

The system integrates robot modeling, retinal environment reconstruction, task-specific interaction, and learning-oriented evaluation.

Ophthalmic RobotDual-arm manipulation with mechanically constrained RCM behavior.
Retinal EnvironmentAnatomical eye structures and multimodal retinal reconstruction for intraocular visualization.
Task-specific PhysicsRigid-body simulation for most tasks and GPU-accelerated MPM for deformable trocar insertion.
Learning PipelineScripted expert data collection, offline imitation learning, and online evaluation.
